Requirement Analysis

1. Project Overview

StudentHub Portal is a web-based portal designed to help students manage their academic activities in one place. Instead of using different websites or asking different departments for information, students can access everything through a single portal. It provides features like class schedules, attendance tracking, study materials, task management, fee details, leave applications, academic progress, and an AI assistant called Campus Buddy.



2. Objective

The main objective of StudentHub Portal is to make student life easier by providing all important academic information on one platform. It helps students save time, stay organized, and quickly access the information they need.



3. Functional Requirements

The system should provide the following features:

Students can log in securely using their username and password.

The dashboard should display important information such as the next class, attendance, today's tasks, and announcements.

Students should be able to view their weekly and daily class schedule.

The Bunk Meter should help students check their attendance and know whether they can miss classes.

The Study Space should provide subject-wise study materials and notes.

Campus Buddy should answer students' questions and provide important information using an AI assistant.

The Task Planner should display pending, upcoming, and completed academic tasks.

Students should be able to view their semester results and subject-wise performance.

The Leave Track module should allow students to apply for leave and check the status of their requests.

The Fee Desk should display fee details, payment history, receipts, and important notices.

The My Profile page should show personal and academic information.




4. Non-Functional Requirements

The portal should be easy to use.

Pages should load quickly.

Student data should remain secure.

The portal should work properly on different web browsers.

The interface should be simple and user-friendly.

7. User Requirements

Students should be able to:

Log in to the portal.

View their dashboard.

Check their class schedule.

Monitor attendance.

Access study materials.

Ask questions through Campus Buddy.

Manage tasks.

View academic progress.

Apply for leave.

Check fee information.

View their profile.




8. Assumptions

Every student has a valid login account.

The college regularly updates attendance, fees, and academic records.

Students have an internet connection to access the portal.




9. Constraints

Only authorized students can access the portal.

Some features depend on the college database being updated.

Internet connection is required to use the portal.




10. Expected Outcome

The StudentHub Portal will provide students with a single platform to manage their academic activities easily. It will help students stay organized, save time, and quickly access important information such as schedules, attendance, study materials, fees, leave status, academic progress, and AI-based support through Campus Buddy.
